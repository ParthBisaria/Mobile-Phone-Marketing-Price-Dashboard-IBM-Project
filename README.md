# Mobile-Phone-Marketing-Price-Dashboard-IBM-Project
## 📱 Mobile Phone Market Pricing Dashboard

A dark-themed dashboard exploring mobile phone pricing, sales volume and customer satisfaction across brands, segments, regions and countries.

![Mobile Phone Market Dashboard](images/mobile-dashboard.png)

### 🔑 At a Glance

| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| **KPI Cards** | Total price, Total units, Average units, Average rating |
| **Bar Chart** | Average price by brand |
| **Area Chart** | Price and units by region |
| **Donut Chart** | Units by segment |
| **Line Chart** | Units by region and segment |
| **Pie Chart** | Average rating by region |
| **Map** | Countries coloured by region |
| **Slicer** | Segment (cross-filters every visual) |

<details>
<summary><b>📂 View all 7 fields</b></summary>

- Brand
- Segment
- Region
- Country
- Avg Price (USD)
- Units Sold (thousands)
- Customer Rating (out of 5)

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🎛️ How to explore it</b></summary>

1. Choose a **Segment** in the slicer.
2. See prices, units, ratings and the map change instantly.
3. Click a region or brand to drill into the details.

</details>
